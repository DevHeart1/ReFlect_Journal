## Core Concept
**Reflective Journaling**: Encourages users to reflect on their day, emotions, and thoughts through guided prompts or free-form entries, with a focus on self-discovery and mindfulness.

---

## Core Features

### User Experience
- **User-Friendly Interface**: An intuitive, clean design that makes journaling effortless and enjoyable.
- **Rich Text Formatting**: Provides options for bold, italics, bullet points, and lists to help users express their thoughts beautifully.

### AI-Powered Reflections
- **Entry Analysis**: Uses AI to analyze journal entries, offering insights and identifying emotional patterns.
- **Smart Prompts**: Suggests reflective questions based on previous entries and behavior.

---

## Multimedia Support
- **Photo and Video Uploads**: Add images and videos for vivid storytelling.
- **Voice Journaling**: Integrates voice-to-text for users who prefer to speak rather than type.

---

## Organization and Insights
- **Tags and Categories**: Categorize entries with keywords for easy organization.
- **Timeline and Calendar View**: Visual representation of journaling history and progress.
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Provides feedback on emotional trends.
- **Mood & Activity Correlation**: Identifies how daily activities influence mood.
- **Mood Tracking**: Track emotional state using emojis, mood wheel, or notes.
